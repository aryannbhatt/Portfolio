Who I am

A jack of all trades, on purpose.

Stories and people. Everything I've done comes back to those two.

I'm at my best in a room full of people: sharing a little joy, listening properly, and helping someone feel heard. I also care about the harder moments, and I'm willing to sit with another person's pain rather than look away. That is why my path has never run down a single lane.

The range is intentional. I've reported for The Hindu, directed and performed theatre, taught in under-resourced classrooms, hosted stages, led CSR drives and built engagement for a 2,500-person firm. The settings change but the work does not: pay attention to people and help their story come out. It also makes me quick to learn and easy to work with, in a newsroom or a boardroom.

I began by writing stories on paper. Today I shape experiences and create stories in person, with people, inside a company. The platform changed. The spirit did not.

Case study · Wellness Club · 8 October 2026

World Mental Health Day at Wissen

What does it really mean to make space for mental well-being at work? I brought psychology, yoga and spirituality into one room to explore it.

My role

Concept to execution, as part of the Wissen Wellness Club. I co-facilitated with Shaifali Verma (Senior Manager, Learning & Development) and partnered with Isha Foundation volunteers, who shared practices such as Nadi Shuddhi and meditation. We also pointed everyone to the free therapy support Wissen offers through 1to1help.

The idea

There is no one-size-fits-all approach to mental well-being. The session helped 100+ attendees manage stress, build self-awareness and find what works for them, in Bengaluru and online from other offices.

Why it matters to me

I studied psychology throughout college, and it's why HR feels meaningful to me. HR isn't only processes and policies; it's about creating places where people feel heard, supported and able to thrive.

100+attendees
60+in person, the rest online
8 Oct 2026World Mental Health Day
Isha Foundationyoga and meditation partner
About the session

Organised by the Wellness Club to bring yoga, meditation and psychology together, and to encourage open conversations about stress, self-awareness, listening and well-being.

  • With Isha Foundation: the mechanics of stress, simple practices for the body-mind connection, perspectives on inner balance, and a guided meditation for daily life
  • Psychology-informed perspective: the Chief of Staff team with Shaifali Verma (Senior Manager, L&D) and me, using interactive games and reflection exercises on listening, expression, self-awareness and feeling heard
The activity I designed

“Leave It in the Basket”

Participants anonymously wrote down one thing getting in the way of their everyday functioning, or stopping them from being fully themselves, folded it and placed it in a basket. Throwing the notes away doesn't make problems disappear, and that was never the point. The first step is acknowledging something is there.

Name, privately and without judgement, the thing that's weighing on you.

“One session cannot solve everything, nor should it pretend to. But it can open a door.”

Service Learning · Christ University · 2024

Media literacy at Karnataka Public School, Adugodi

With teammates Alina Tom and Swastika Soni, I introduced media literacy to students in grades 8 and 9 at a public school where the infrastructure was barely holding up, but the students' determination stood tall.

We started with newspapers and digital tools. The sessions grew into conversations about the internet, responsible tech use and possibilities the students hadn't imagined, and ended with them designing their own student magazine.
